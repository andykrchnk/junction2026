Methods, limitations & downloads

All geometry comparisons retain source-image coordinates. Previous and current refinement use the same 2048 forward output. The same 0.01% page-area filter is used for predictions and CAD references. New outlines use model walls and openings to split the footprint; CAD is evaluation only.

Only three drawings support CAD geometry scoring. Other results are qualitative. CubiCasa-domain samples may overlap training data. Model names and class scores are unverified. Review heuristics do not catch every merge.
